PatentRef for agents

An agent can get a claim tree, a brief, a status record, a search over US grants, the patents nearest to a text, rows of patent office rules and the build facts of the data it is reading, each answer stamped with X-Data-Version. Nothing PatentRef returns is a legal conclusion; every answer describes the text as printed.
